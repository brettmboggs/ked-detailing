## Job 1: Point your website address to the new site

⏱ 5 minutes · 💻 Use a computer · 📱 Keep your phone close for a code

Your old website keeps working until this is done.
If you ever need to undo it, Brett can put it back.
Your email won't change.

1. Go to **account.squarespace.com** and log in.
2. Click **Domains**.
3. Click **kedservice.com**.
4. Click **DNS**.
5. Click **Domain Nameservers**.
6. Click **Use custom nameservers**.
7. If it asks for your password, type it in.
8. If it asks for a code, check your phone and type the code in.
9. If you see a warning, click **Continue**.
10. You'll see two empty boxes. Type these in exactly:
    - Box 1: **georgia.ns.cloudflare.com**
    - Box 2: **lou.ns.cloudflare.com**
11. Click **Save**.

✅ **Done.** Tell Brett. The new site shows up within a few hours.

⚠️ **Don't cancel Squarespace yet.** Brett will tell you when.

## Job 3: Set up Stripe so customers can pay you

⏱ 15 minutes · 💻 Use a computer

Stripe is how you'll get paid by card. The money goes to your Commerce bank account.

**Have these next to you before you start:**
- 🧾 Your business tax number (**EIN**). It's on letters from the IRS. If you don't have one, your Social Security number works.
- 🏦 A **check** from your Commerce business account. Its bottom row has the two numbers Stripe asks for.
- 🪪 Your **driver's license**.

1. Go to **stripe.com**.
2. Click **Start now**.
3. Type your email, your name and a password.
4. Open your email and click the link from Stripe.
5. Stripe asks about your business. Use these answers:
   - Business type: **LLC**
   - Legal name: **Knock Em Down Auto & Marine Detailing LLC**
   - Website: **kedservice.com**
   - What you sell: **Mobile auto and boat detailing**
6. Type in your EIN when it asks.
7. Type in your own name, birthday and home address. (Every business has to do this. Customers never see it.)
8. For the bank, type the **routing number** and **account number** from your check.
9. Click **Submit**.

**Last part: let Brett in, so he never needs your password.**

10. Click **Settings**. It's the ⚙️ gear.
11. Click **Team**.
12. Click **+ New member**.
13. Type **brettmboggs@gmail.com**.
14. Pick **Developer**.
15. Click **Invite**.

✅ **Done.** Tell Brett.

## Job 4: Send Brett your bank history

⏱ 5 minutes · 💻 Use a computer

This lets Brett test the new money tracking with your real numbers.
He can only **see** them. Nothing can move your money.

1. Go to **commercebank.com** and log in.
2. Click your **business checking** account.
3. Look for **Download** or **Export**. It's usually near the top of the list.
4. For the file type, pick **Quicken** (it may say **.QFX**).
5. For the dates, pick **the last 90 days**.
6. Click **Download**.
7. Email that file to **brettmboggs@gmail.com**.
8. If your business **credit card** is also with Commerce, do the same for it.

✅ **Done.**
